<p style="text-align: justify;">
<i>Attack of the Friday Monsters! A Tokyo Tale</i> is a love letter to the past, to childhood, and to those carefree summer vacations. Lately my days have been pretty hectic, and I had a lot to take care of, so this game turned out to be a great choice to unwind. It is surprising that even with its slow pace, it never bored me, and it felt good to wander around the hand-painted town and find out what these monsters actually are and why they are causing trouble every single Friday.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
I still don’t understand why this game has never received a physical release. It would have easily fit on a cartridge as part of the <i>Guild02</i> bundle with the other titles. It is also interesting that back in the DS era they released every piece of junk, but that really dropped off with the 3DS. Maybe the touchscreen lost its charm, or maybe developing for the system was harder and you could not make a quick buck from shovelware anymore. Meanwhile, <i>Teddy Together</i> did get a release. I have no idea why I even own that game, and on top of that, it is the Italian version.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
I really liked Kaz Ayabe’s style in this game, and I already found another title that builds on a similar summer vacation vibe. It is <i>Boku no Natsuyasumi</i>, and three of its entries are available on the PSP. Maybe this is a sign that it is time for me to buy one. Say no more!
</p>
